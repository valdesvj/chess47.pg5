Keys
SHIFTthe game menu on the soft keys (Shift pressed alone, or the SHIFT button): F1 XIANGQI / CHESS (each game is kept, with its clock), F2 PIECES, F3 ZOOM, F4 RESIGN, F5 DRAW, F6 THINK
THINKSHIFT F6: the computer's thinking on / off. While it thinks: depth, best move, score; the expected line; positions searched, a second, time; its move marked on the board
CLOCKsoft-key page 2 (F2): OFF, 1+0, 3+2, 5+3, 10+5, 15+10, 25+10, 90+30 (3+2 FIDE blitz, 15+10 FIDE rapid, 90+30 classical), before the first move. During a game CLOCK only stops the clock (twice); OFF shows each side's time used. The next control at NEW
click / tappick a piece, then its square (castling: the king, then its rook)
* or xthe pieces: chess figures / letters; Xiangqi characters / symbols / letters
+ or -zoom: the board the full height, no soft keys (they still work)
F1 … F6the soft keys under the screen (or click them); >> the next page
8 2 4 6, arrowsthe cursor; 7 9 1 3 diagonally
5, Enterpick / play at the cursor
Esccancel; while the computer thinks: move now
Backspacetake back (two plies against the computer); Shift+Backspace resign at once
RESIGN, DRAWsoft-key page 2 (F4, F5; RESIGN twice). The computer accepts a draw when it is worse, or from move 20 when it is not better. With 2 players the other side sees ACCEPT on its turn (press twice); a move declines. NEW in an unfinished game against the computer counts as resigning (NEW twice; NEW again for the next game)
iinfo: the engine's last search

Xiangqi (Chinese chess, SHIFT F1): the same levels, clock and modes; moves in WXF notation (C2=5, H8+7). Soft keys: NEW LEVEL FLIP UNDO HINT / PIECES CLOCK SIDE RESIGN DRAW / CHESS MODE FEN. No legal move loses (stalemate too); repetition is a draw; 60 moves without a capture a draw. And SHIFT 7 (七, seven)…

Levels CRAZY, 1-9 as on the calculator. With DM42n speed the engine searches as many positions as the DM42n on USB power would in the same time (about 19 000 a second), so the levels keep the calculator's strength (level 9 about 2480 against Stockfish). The game is kept in this browser and resumed.
