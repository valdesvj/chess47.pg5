Keys
click / tappick a piece, then its square (castling: the king, then its rook)
F1 … F6the soft keys under the screen (or click them); >> the next page
8 2 4 6, arrowsthe cursor; 7 9 1 3 diagonally
5, Enterpick / play at the cursor
Esccancel; while the computer thinks: move now
Backspacetake back (two plies against the computer); Shift+Backspace resign at once
RESIGN, DRAWsoft-key page 2 (F4, F5; RESIGN twice). The computer accepts a draw when it is worse, or from move 20 when it is not better. With 2 players the other side sees ACCEPT on its turn (press twice); a move declines. NEW in an unfinished game against the computer counts as resigning (NEW twice; NEW again for the next game)
iinfo: the engine's last search

Levels CRAZY, 1-9 as on the calculator. With DM42n speed the engine searches as many positions as the DM42n on USB power would in the same time (about 19 000 a second), so the levels keep the calculator's strength (level 9 about 2480 against Stockfish). The game is kept in this browser and resumed.
